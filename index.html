About

I'm a Hardware Systems Engineer at Zoox on the Hardware Software Integration team, where I build internal tooling, diagnostics, and provisioning systems that help engineers and manufacturing teams work with autonomous vehicles more efficiently. Before this I spent time at Ford in Advanced EV Development managing a HIL (LabCar) test environment, and earlier roles at Zoox spanning manufacturing operations, diagnostics, and test engineering — so I've got a grounded, hands-on view of how hardware and software actually have to work together at production scale, not just in theory. I'm finishing an M.S. in Computer Science at Oregon State University (expected Spring 2027), building on a B.S. in Electrical Engineering — Power and Control Systems — from Cal State Long Beach.

Personal — OSU HCI Capstone

A communication device for my dogs

A three-part connected system built the same way I approach hardware at work — layered, testable, and designed around the people (or dogs) actually using it. PawPress is the physical push-button hardware prototype. PawSignal is the app layer that receives and routes each signal. PawHome closes the loop by triggering a home-automation response, turning a single button press into an automated action in the house.

I validated the design with structured usability and inclusivity walkthroughs — real users, real friction, caught before it shipped — the same heuristic-evaluation discipline I'd apply to a factory test rig.
