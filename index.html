About

I'm a Hardware Systems Engineer at Zoox on the Hardware Software Integration team, where I build internal tooling, diagnostics, and provisioning systems that help engineers and manufacturing teams work with autonomous vehicles more efficiently. Before this I was at Ford in Advanced EV Development, plus a few earlier roles at Zoox spanning manufacturing operations and test & diagnostics — so I've got a pretty grounded, hands-on view of how hardware and software actually have to work together at scale, not just in theory. I'm finishing an MS in Computer Science at Oregon State University right now, working toward platform engineering roles in robotics and autonomy. Outside of work I bring the same instinct to hands-on projects — home renovation, building a communication device for my dogs — and I'm generally drawn to work that makes life easier for the people actually operating complex systems day to day.
